About the treatment

Assessment comes first

Recent injuries should first receive proper medical assessment. Traditional supportive care may then be selected according to the diagnosis, swelling, movement and stage of recovery.

Treatment workflow

1

Safety Check

Identify whether urgent imaging or hospital care is required.

2

Assessment

Review pain, swelling, movement and medical findings.

3

Supportive Care

Use suitable external care, rest and stabilisation guidance.

4

Rehabilitation

Review healing and gradually support safe movement.

Important: Severe pain, deformity, numbness, bleeding or loss of movement requires emergency medical care.
