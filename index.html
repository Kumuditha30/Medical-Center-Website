About SUWAYU

Healing that respects your body, mind and way of life.

SUWAYU Ayurveda Medical Center is envisioned as a welcoming centre for authentic Ayurvedic treatment and holistic wellness. Every patient begins with a detailed consultation so that the care plan can reflect their individual needs, lifestyle and health concerns.

Our environment is designed to feel peaceful, clean and private. Traditional therapies, herbal preparations, dietary guidance and practical lifestyle recommendations are brought together to support sustainable wellbeing.
