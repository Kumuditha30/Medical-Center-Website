Nasya Therapy

About the treatment

Practitioner-selected nasal care

Nasya uses a selected preparation in a controlled quantity. The type and timing depend on symptoms, health condition and suitability, so it should be performed under professional guidance.

Treatment workflow

1

Consultation

Review symptoms, allergies, medicines and health conditions.

2

Preparation

Gentle facial care or warming may be used when suitable.

3

Nasya Procedure

The selected preparation is administered under supervision.

4

Aftercare

Rest briefly and follow the practitioner’s instructions.

Nasya should not be self-administered without guidance from a qualified practitioner.
