About the treatment

A supervised wellness programme

Panchakarma is not one treatment. It includes preparation, selected therapies and a recovery stage. The duration and procedures are decided only after consultation.

Treatment workflow

1

Assessment

Review constitution, digestion, strength and present condition.

2

Preparation

Use suitable food, oils or warming therapies to prepare the body.

3

Main Therapy

Complete only the procedures recommended by the practitioner.

4

Recovery

Follow a gradual diet, rest plan and follow-up guidance.
